Architecture Overview

  • • Multi-Hop Graph Model: Maps complex village topology (PtP microwave relays, solar towers, APs) into Neo4j graph store with instant in-memory fallback.
  • • Deterministic Diagnostics: Normalized composite scoring (loss, latency, throughput, link up/down) with upstream failure suppression.
  • • Subscriber Impact Tracing: Traverses network dependencies in real time to isolate affected local businesses, schools, and clinics.
  • • Sarvam Regional AI: Combines sarvam-105b and Bulbul-v1 neural voice in 7 Indian languages for non-technical local operators.
  • • Village Business Hub: Integrates automated network health with NPCI UPI billing, payment tracking, and WhatsApp dispatch.
